# Opportunity Scoring

Score X records to prioritize growth opportunities.

| Factor | Score | Signal |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Purchase intent | 0-5 | Buying, evaluating, switching, asking for recommendations |
| Pain intensity | 0-5 | Clear frustration, workaround, urgency, missing feature |
| Audience fit | 0-5 | Matches the product's target user, buyer, or use case |
| Recency | 0-3 | Recent posts and active threads score higher |
| Engagement | 0-3 | Replies, reposts, likes, quote posts, or discussion depth |

Use the score to rank records, not as an absolute truth. Summarize the evidence behind each high-priority signal.

Total opportunity score = purchase intent + pain intensity + audience fit + recency + engagement.

Maximum score: 21. Use tie-breakers in this order: stronger source evidence, better audience fit, more recent activity.
